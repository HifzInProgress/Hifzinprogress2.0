About the Project

Walking the Path

Hifz In Progress was born out of a desire to document the reality of Qur'an memorization for modern students. Not as an accomplished scholar, but as an earnest student striving each morning to carry the Book of Allah in the heart.

Our Core Mission

Why Hifz In Progress Exists

Many young Muslims yearn to memorize the Qur'an, but feel overwhelmed by traditional narratives that portray Hifz as an impossible sprint reserved only for childhood prodigies. We believe that with the right methodology, a sincere heart, and consistent daily dedication, any Muslim can embark on this sacred journey.

Through transparent daily logs, honest reflections on difficulties, and structured tools, we aim to demystify the process and build a supportive community of lifelong Quran companions.

1. Sincerity Over Status

Guard against the temptation to show off or seek the title of “Hafiz”. Every letter is recited for Allah's countenance alone.

2. Consistency Over Speed

Five verses memorized like steel with rigorous daily Sabqi revision beats two rushed pages that evaporate overnight.

3. Teacher Rigor

We strongly uphold the prophetic tradition of learning directly from a qualified Sanad-holding Sheikh, never self-memorizing in isolation.
