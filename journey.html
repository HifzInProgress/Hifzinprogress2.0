“And We will make your way easy.”
Surah Al-A'la (87:8)
Day 1 · Started

The Pure Intention (Niyyah) & Day 1

The journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step. I purchased a standard 15-line Madani Mus'haf and spent the first morning renewing my intention: this memorization is solely for the pleasure of Allah, free from vanity or seeking titles.

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا
“My Lord, increase me in knowledge.” — Surah Taha (20:114)
Day 2 · Routine

Establishing The Golden Fajr Hour

Woke up 45 minutes before Fajr. The silence of dawn is unmatched: zero notifications, pure cognitive freshness. Memorized 7 lines and recited them in Sunnah prayer.

إِنَّ قُرْآنَ الْفَجْرِ كَانَ مَشْهُودًا
“Indeed, the recitation of dawn is ever witnessed.” — Surah Al-Isra (17:78)
Day 3 · Guidance

Connecting With A Qualified Sheikh

Met with Sheikh Yusuf for my initial Nazrah audit. He immediately caught subtle articulation slips in my heavy letters (Kha and Qaf). Lesson learned: Never memorize an ayah before reciting it to a qualified teacher.

وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا
“And recite the Qur'an with measured, rhythmic recitation.” — Surah Al-Muzzammil (73:4)
Day 4 · Progress

Completing Juz 'Amma (Juz 30)

Recited all 37 surahs from An-Naba to An-Nas in one continuous sitting before the Sheikh. An emotional milestone, but the teacher advised: “Now the true test begins: holding onto it through daily Sabqi revision.”

Days 5 & 6 · Consistency & Focus

The Battle of Sabqi & Sanctuary Setup

Removed social media apps from my study phone. Sitting in a dedicated quiet prayer corner with a wooden rehal increased retention by at least 50%. Steady consistency beats erratic volume every single time.

Day 7+ Ongoing Updates

The journey continues every single day. New reflections, video clips, and teacher notes are added as we progress through Juz 29 and beyond.
