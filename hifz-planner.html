Golden Rules for This Timetable

1. Consistency Beats Quantity: Memorizing 5 lines every single day without failing is exponentially superior to memorizing 2 pages sporadically.

2. One Single Mus'haf: Never switch to a different print or font size. Visual photographic memory locks the position of verses on the physical page.

3. Sabaq Can Wait, Revision Cannot: If you are tired or short on time, reduce or pause your new Sabaq, but never skip Sabqi or Manzil.
